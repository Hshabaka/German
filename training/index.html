Squat

  • MusclesGlutes, thighs, calves, abs
  • Reps10-15
  • EquipmentNo equipment

Feet just outside shoulder width, hips back and down, drive through the heels, abs braced.

Knee push-up

  • MusclesChest, shoulders, arms
  • Reps10-15
  • EquipmentNo equipment

Hands slightly in front of the shoulders, lower the chest toward the floor, push back up, abs in.

Reverse lunge

  • MusclesGlutes, thighs
  • Reps10-15 each leg
  • EquipmentNo equipment

Step back, front knee bent to 90°, keep abs tight, return to standing.

Dumbbell biceps curl

  • MusclesBiceps (arms)
  • Reps10-15
  • EquipmentDumbbells

Feet hip-width, elbows tucked in, curl toward the collarbone, lower under control.

Start with a weight or level you can do comfortably. Stop if anything hurts.
